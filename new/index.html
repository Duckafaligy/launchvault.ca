Questions

Where do your numbers actually come from?
Revenue comes from TrustMRR, which reads it straight from Stripe or RevenueCat. We add GitHub, the Wayback Machine, interviews and the founder's own posts, and link every source with the date we read it.
What's the difference between Verified and Claimed?
Verified means we read it from a live source, like Stripe through TrustMRR, on the date shown. Claimed means the founder or an interview said it, and we link where. Sources differ means two sources disagree, so we show both. Couldn't verify means a claim has no source either way, and we say so.
Do I need money or followers to get something out of this?
No. The stories are picked so you can copy a move with little money and no audience. Each breakdown ends with a checklist that fits around school.
I'm under 18. Can I still use it?
Yes, it's made for 15 to 20 year olds. Free needs only an email. If you're under 18, a parent pays for Pro or Developer.
Is any of this financial advice?
No. LaunchVault explains what real founders did, using public sources. Nothing here is financial or investment advice.
