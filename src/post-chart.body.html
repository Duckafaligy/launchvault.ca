How to read a revenue chart

MRR, all-time revenue, and what a sudden jump on the line actually means. One real chart, three questions to ask.

GuidesLaunchVault5 min readNov 17 2026
In short
  • Check what the line measures: one month of subscriptions (MRR), or everything ever earned (all-time).
  • Check which points are verified. On most charts, only some are.
  • When the line jumps, look for what the founder did just before.

    1. What does the line measure?

    Monthly recurring revenue (MRR) is what subscriptions bring in each month. Postiz's MRR was $430,896 on November 17, 2026 Verified. All-time revenue adds up everything that ever came in: $1,946,080 for Postiz Verified.

    The two aren't comparable. Stencil AI reports all-time revenue ($483,794), so putting it on a monthly chart next to Postiz would make no sense. Always check the label on the axis.

    2. Which points are checked?

    On the Postiz chart, only the last point is verified, read from Stripe by TrustMRR. The earlier points come from the founder's posts and a case study, so they're claimed.

    One month has two numbers. For March 2026, a case study says $21K, but the founder said $45,000 a month in an interview Sources differ. The chart uses $21K and shows $45K as a second point, so you can see both.

    3. What happened just before the jump?

    The flat part matters as much as the jump. After going open source in September 2024, Postiz went from about $700 a month to $14K by November 2025 Claimed: over a year of slow growth.

    Then the line jumps: $21K in March 2026, $80K in April, $118K in June Claimed. Move 6 on the timeline explains it. OpenClaw, an AI agent project, went viral, and Postiz was already plugged into it. The work from 2025 is what let it catch the 2026 wave.

    Not every jump has an answer. Revenue went from $186,713 in September 2026 Claimed to $430,896 in November Verified, and we couldn't find a public explanation yet.

    When we can't explain a jump, we say so.

    Three questions for any revenue chart

    • Is it monthly or all-time?
    • Which points are verified, and on what date were they read?
    • What did the founder do just before the line moved?
