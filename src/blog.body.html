Rezi started as a resume template on Reddit and made $216K in October 2026

A free template, four slow years, then an early bet on AI writing. October 2026 brought in $216,471, read straight from Stripe. That is about 21% less than April 2026 ($275,551), and revenue has dropped every month since. Rezi's MRR (monthly recurring revenue) is $234,707: what all current subscriptions add up to per month, while $216,471 is the money actually collected in October.
