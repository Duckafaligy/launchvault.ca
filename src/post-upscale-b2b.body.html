Upscale B2B sold $3 email inboxes to cold-email agencies, then became one, and now makes about $99K a month

It started by selling the 'picks and shovels' of cold email (the tools everyone doing it needs): ready-to-send inboxes for a few dollars a month. Then it started running campaigns itself, with a free-to-try pilot. Its MRR (monthly recurring revenue: what customers pay every month) is $140,324, read straight from Stripe. That is higher than October's revenue, the money actually collected that month, so the two numbers don't match.

The story, phase by phase

Each phase ends with one lesson you can use.

1Fixing their own problem2023 to 2024

Cold email means emailing businesses that don't know you yet. To send a lot without landing in spam, agencies (companies that do this work for clients) need many 'warmed up' inboxes: new inboxes used gently at first so email providers trust them. The company's site says it had been burned by bad inbox providers, 'That's why we started Upscale B2B'. TrustMRR lists March 2023 as the founding date.

Founded Mar 31 2023 Claimed

A problem that annoys you at work is often a problem other people will pay to make go away.

2Selling inboxes to agenciesDec 2024 to 2025

The site sold Google Workspace and Microsoft inboxes, set up and warmed, for $2 to $7 a month each, with a free domain (a web address). Bigger buyers got lower prices: $2.50 each at 100 inboxes, $2 at 1,000. A sales calculator showed how many deals more inboxes could bring.

$2 to $7 per inbox per month Wayback

Selling to businesses that already spend money on a problem is easier than convincing someone they have one.

3A separate brand for inboxesApr to Nov 2025

The inbox business got its own site, warminboxes.com, first archived in April 2025 with 'Get FREE Warmed-Up Domains'. By November 2025 its headline promised '90%+ Deliverability' (the share of emails that land in the inbox, not spam). The founder's TrustMRR account is named after it.

"Get FREE Warmed-Up Domains" Wayback

If one product sells itself, give it its own name and page so people can find it.

4Running campaigns, try firstJan 2026 to now

By January 2026 upscaleb2b.com led with done-for-you lead generation (finding possible customers for a business) and a trial: 'the only provider offering a trial for fully managed lead generation'. It runs a small pilot campaign first, then grows it if it works. Revenue went from about $34K in December 2025 to $99K in October 2026.

$99,409 in Oct 2026 Verified

Letting customers try before they commit can win clients who would never sign a big contract cold.
