Community Guidelines

The short version
  • Be kind. Disagree with ideas, not people.
  • Never share personal info, yours or anyone else's.
  • Keep the conversation here: no contact details, no chat-app or social profile links.
  • No selling, ads or asking for money.
  • See something wrong? Report it with the … menu. Don't argue back.

Who can post

The community chat is part of the LaunchVault app. It is for people aged 13 and up, and it is built mainly for members aged 13 to 20 who want to talk about real startup stories and their own small projects.

  • You need an account. You have to be signed in to read or post. People who aren't signed in can't see any messages or threads.
  • You need to be 13 or older. Under 13s can't use the app. Ages are self-declared, so we can't check them. Please tell the truth.
  • You pick a chat name and agree to these guidelines before your first post. Your name shows with a 4-digit tag, so nobody can pass as someone else. You can change your name up to 3 times a day.
  • It's all public to members. There are no private messages. Everything you post can be seen by every signed-in member, including members under 18.
  • Text only. You can't post photos or files right now.

The five rules

1. Be kind. Disagree with ideas, not people. Strong opinions are welcome, especially in polls. Attacks on a person are not.

  • OK: "I don't think that pricing would work. Most people won't pay $20 for that."
  • OK: "I voted B. Here's why A worries me."
  • Not OK: insults, slurs, threats, mocking someone, or ganging up on one member.
  • Not OK: sexual comments about anyone, or telling someone to hurt themselves.

2. No personal info. Don't share your school, address, phone number, email or social accounts. Don't share anyone else's either.

  • OK: "I'm in high school in Ontario."
  • Not OK: your full name with your school, your street, a phone number or an email address.
  • Not OK: passwords or API keys, even by accident in code you paste.

3. Keep it here. No contact details, no "DM me", and no links to chat apps or social profiles. Conversations stay where everyone can see them.

  • OK: "Start a thread under Ask for help and I'll answer there."
  • Not OK: "add me on snap", "dms open", an @handle, or a Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, Instagram or TikTok link.

4. No selling. No ads, promo codes, referral links or asking for money.

  • OK: "I launched my first app this week. Here's what I learned from the first 10 users."
  • Not OK: "Use my code for 20% off", "get rich quick" offers, or asking members to send money.

5. Report, don't argue. If a message breaks a rule, use the … menu on it and choose Report. Replying to fight back usually makes things worse.

Staying safe

  • Never share personal info, even with someone who seems friendly.
  • Be careful with anyone who asks your age, where you live or go to school, asks for photos, offers gifts or money, or wants to talk somewhere else or "keep it secret". Report them and tell a parent or an adult you trust.
  • Sexual content involving minors and grooming are never allowed. Our automated check always hides messages that look like grooming, and flags them so a person looks first. We remove this content, can ban the account, and, as our terms of use say, we can report illegal content to the authorities.
  • If someone talks about hurting themselves, the message is hidden and flagged so a person looks at it first. If you or a friend are in danger right now, call 911. In Canada you can also call or text 9-8-8, or reach Kids Help Phone at 1-800-668-6868 (text 686868), any time.
  • Links: when you tap a link, we show a "You're leaving LaunchVault" warning first. We haven't checked what's on the other side. Never type a password or card number on a site you reached from chat.

How moderation works

Two things check messages: automatic filters when you post, and an automated check right after. People look at anything flagged and make the final decisions on bans.

When you post, filters stop the message from sending if it has:

  • an email address, a phone number, an @handle, or phrases like "dm me" or "add me on…";
  • words on our blocked list;
  • a link that isn't allowed: shortened links, links to chat apps or social profiles, sites not on our list of known sites (for now), or more than 2 links. New members can post links after a day and a few messages.

There are also limits on how fast you can post, so nobody can flood a channel.

Right after you post, an AI model (Claude, made by Anthropic) reads each new message, along with the channel and the few messages before it, and sorts it. This usually happens within a minute or two. It can:

  • do nothing, for normal conversation;
  • send it to a person to look at, when it might be a problem. The message stays up meanwhile;
  • hide it, when it clearly breaks a rule. Messages that look like grooming or self-harm are always hidden and flagged so a person looks at them first;
  • pause posting for 24 hours, if 3 of your messages are hidden this way in 7 days. That pause is marked for a person to review, who keeps it or lifts it.

The AI can make mistakes. Every automated action is logged, and a person can undo it. If the check can't run, your message stays up, and after a few failed tries it goes to a person instead. Only people decide on longer or permanent bans. How we handle the information involved is in the privacy policy.

Reporting

  • Tap the … menu on any message from another member and choose Report. Pick a reason (Mean, Unsafe, Spam, Personal info or Other) and add a note if you want.
  • The message is hidden for you straight away.
  • Your report goes to the LaunchVault team's review list. We look at every report.
  • If 3 different members report the same message, it is hidden for everyone until a person looks at it.
  • The person you reported isn't told who reported them.

What happens if you break a rule

We pick the smallest step that fixes the problem:

  • The message is removed. It shows as "This message was removed" for everyone.
  • A temporary pause. You can still read everything, but you can't post, start threads or vote for a while, like the 24-hour pause above.
  • A ban. For serious or repeated problems, a person can stop you posting for longer or for good, and remove your messages.
  • Your account. Breaking our terms of use, for example lying about your age, can mean we close the account.

You can always delete your own messages from the … menu.

Appeals and contact

Think we got it wrong? Email [contact email] with your chat name and 4-digit tag, the channel, roughly when it happened, and why you think it was a mistake. A person will look again and reply within [7] days. Parents can contact us the same way about their child's account.

Changes to these guidelines

If we change these guidelines, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is important, we'll tell account holders by email before it applies.
