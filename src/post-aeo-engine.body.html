The story, phase by phase.

Each phase ends with one lesson you can use.

1An Amazon agency2018 to 2024

The business began as FosterFBA, which helped people sell on Amazon. FBA means Fulfilled by Amazon: Amazon stores and ships the seller's products. In January 2019 its homepage offered product research, listing optimization and free tools. By 2023 it called itself a 'Data Driven Amazon Advertising Agency' and offered a free audit to get calls booked.

"Research, Optimize & Scale your Amazon FBA Business" Wayback

A small service business can run for years and build skills you can carry into the next thing.

2Spotting the new search2025

People started asking ChatGPT and Google's AI answers instead of clicking search results. The company says it was already doing AI search work through 2025. By October 2025 aeoengine.ai was live, promising to get brands 'featured in ChatGPT, Google AI & Perplexity answers' and offering a free audit.

aeoengine.ai first archived Oct 30 2025 Wayback

When the way people search changes, the people who already know the old way have a head start on the new one.

3A new nameJan 2026

The company says it 'formalized' the AEO Engine brand around January 2026. AEO means answer engine optimization: making sure an AI recommends you when someone asks a question. It is like SEO (getting found on Google), but for AI answers. That month a founder-story site listed it at $65,000 a month, which matches the Stripe numbers.

$65,126 in Jan 2026 Verified

A clear name for what you do can matter as much as the work itself.

4Showing up everywhereMar to Apr 2026

In March 2026 it started a podcast about AI search, now billed as a daily show. In April the founder gave the AEO talk at a big Amazon seller conference in Nashville, where the agency's old clients gather. Revenue jumped to $98K in March, then settled around $75K to $77K a month.

$98,264 in Mar 2026 Verified

Go where your existing customers already are and teach them something new.

5The jumpSep to Oct 2026

Revenue went from about $80K in August to $164K in September and $204K in October. Plans are public: $1,597 or $2,997 a month, or custom from $10,000. In November it published an 'Answer Index' ranking how visible 100 law firms are in AI answers. We couldn't find a public reason for the September jump.

$204,043 in Oct 2026 Verified

Public prices and free reports let buyers say yes faster.
