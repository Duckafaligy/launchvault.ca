Rezi started as a resume template on Reddit and made $216K in October 2026

A free template, four slow years, then an early bet on AI writing. October 2026 brought in $216,471, read straight from Stripe. That is about 21% less than April 2026 ($275,551), and revenue has dropped every month since. Rezi's MRR (monthly recurring revenue) is $234,707: what all current subscriptions add up to per month, while $216,471 is the money actually collected in October.

The story, phase by phase.

Each phase ends with one lesson you can use.

1A template on Reddit2015

The founder graduated with low grades but still got interviews at big companies, and put that down to his resume. In May 2015 he posted a resume template built to pass hiring software (called ATS) on Reddit so others could use it. The first thing he sold was that template as a Word file with a short guide.

about $9.69 per template Claimed

Share something genuinely useful for free first; the people who use it tell you what to build.

2Four slow years2016 to 2019

The founder moved to South Korea and taught English to pay the bills. A bare-bones tool called Rezi Instant reformatted people's text into the template, with no logins and no database. He spent 2015 to 2018 looking for money and paid an agency to build the real software, which he later called a mistake.

about 3.5 years of building in Korea Claimed

A tiny version that works today beats a big version you are waiting for someone else to build.

3Launch as a paid appSep 2019 to 2020

Rezi launched as a web app with accounts and a paid upgrade in September 2019. Two months later the founder did an AMA (ask me anything) on Reddit, which he calls 'the most authentic channel'. A deal on AppSumo, a site that sells cheap lifetime deals on software, brought in users during the pandemic. StackCommerce, another deals site, brought in revenue.

Launched September 2019 Claimed

Go back to the place your first users came from and talk to them in public.

4First in line for AI writingDec 2020 to 2022

When OpenAI released GPT-3, Rezi used it to write resume lines for users, while the team was just two people. By March 2021 the homepage said 'POWERED BY GPT-3' and 'THE FIRST GTP-3 POWERED RESUME BUILDER', typo included.

"Create Your Resume With Rezi. AI-Powered ATS Resume Builder" Wayback

When a new tool appears, ask what boring part of your product it could do for the user.

5The ChatGPT wave2023

When ChatGPT made everyone curious about AI, Rezi was already an AI resume builder. The company says it hit $1M a year in recurring revenue in March 2023 and 1 million users in July 2023. In January 2023 the plans were Free, $3 a month and $29 a month.

$1M ARR (yearly recurring revenue) in March 2023 Claimed

Be ready before the wave arrives; you can't build fast enough once it does.

6Search, not ads2024 to now

The founder says Rezi spent about $15K a month on paid ads for a year without making the money back, then stopped. Growth came from search results instead: Rezi ranks for 'AI Resume Builder'. He also removed the $3 plan, which he says made each customer worth more. But since April 2026 revenue has fallen every month, from $275,551 to $216,471 in October, about 21% less. We couldn't find a public reason.

$216,471 revenue in Oct 2026 Verified

If ads don't pay for themselves after a fair test, stop and put that money into something that keeps paying off over time, like search. Then keep checking your numbers, because growth can stall.
