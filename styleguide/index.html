Chrome

The nav, the closing panel and the footer are stamped onto every page by build_new.py. You never write them.

  • Navbar. Full bar at the top. Scroll and it shrinks into a floating glass pill. Hover or tap the pill and it grows into a card with the tabs. Esc closes it.
  • Page changes. Moving between pages cross-fades with a small rise; the logo and the nav stay put. Browsers without view transitions simply load the page.
  • First visit. The logo mark draws once per session (350ms), then the page rises in.
  • Background. One band of dots winds down the whole page behind everything. Set "bg": "off" or "bg_dim": 0.75 in the page meta.
  • Closing panel. Below, on every page unless the meta says "end_cta": false.
