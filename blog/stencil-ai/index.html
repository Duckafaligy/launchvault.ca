The story, phase by phase

Each phase ends with one lesson you can use.

  1. 1

    A problem in the family

    Before 2026

    Before every tattoo, artists trace the design by hand into a clean line drawing called a stencil, then transfer it onto skin. The team behind Stencil AI is two brothers. One is the programmer, the other is a tattoo artist, so the problem was sitting right next to the person who could code a fix.

    "Two passionate brothers"WaybackFeb 1 2026

    LessonThe best problems to solve are ones a friend or family member deals with every week.

  2. 2

    Web first, paid from the start

    Sep 2025 to Feb 2026

    The website was already online by September 2025. In February 2026 the homepage promised "a perfect stencil in 30 seconds" and there was no free plan: $14, $29 or $69 a month. The phone app was still marked "Coming soon". In January 2026 it already brought in about $16,000.

    $16,032 in Jan 2026VerifiedNov 18 2026

    LessonYou don't need an app store listing to start charging. A simple website with a price tests whether people will pay.

  3. 3

    Selling to professionals

    2026

    Stencil AI is sold to people who earn money with it. A Studio plan charges per artist, so a whole tattoo shop can pay for it. The pitch is time: the October 2026 homepage says it "replaces hours of tracing in Procreate", a drawing app.

    $29/month Pro, $69/month StudioWaybackOct 11 2026

    LessonPeople pay more easily for a tool that saves them time at work than for one that is just fun.

  4. 4

    The App Store launch

    Mar to Jul 2026

    The iPhone app went live on March 12, 2026, with an Android version too. Monthly revenue grew every month from January to July, from about $16,000 to about $62,000.

    $62,455 in Jul 2026VerifiedNov 18 2026

    LessonOnce the website proves people pay, an app puts the tool where the customer works: on a phone or tablet at the chair.

  5. 5

    Holding steady and getting found

    Aug 2026 to now

    Since July, revenue has stayed between about $60,000 and $64,000 a month. The site has a blog of how-to guides, like how to print a stencil or make one in Procreate, so artists searching Google land on it. Its MRR was $48,693 when we read it, lower than October's $62,976. Both numbers are real but measure different things: MRR only counts subscriptions active on the day it was read, as a monthly amount, while a month's revenue is all the money collected that month, which can include yearly plans paid up front. The TrustMRR listing names app store search, SEO (getting found on Google), a blog, TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest and YouTube as its channels.

    $62,976 in Oct 2026VerifiedNov 18 2026

    LessonWrite the guide your customer is already searching for, and put your tool inside it.
