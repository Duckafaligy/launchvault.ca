The story, phase by phase

Each phase ends with one lesson you can use.

1YouTube first2016 to 2019

One of the co-founders started a motivational speech channel on YouTube in 2016 as a side hobby. One of his first videos got a million views in its first month. He built more channels after that, including one for students.

1 million views in the first month Claimed

An audience you build for free can become the first customers of anything you make later.

2Testing quietlyJan to Nov 2020

The app went on the App Store in January 2020, but the team didn't announce it yet. Thousands of listeners tested it for months first. By then the team had deals with more than 20 YouTube channels to use their speeches, and a royalty system to pay those creators.

500+ audios at launch Claimed

Let real users test before the big launch. Fix what they hate while few people are watching.

3The official launchNov 2020

On November 15, 2020, the team announced Mindset as an app from "the world's biggest motivational YouTube network". They said the partner channels had over 13 million subscribers combined. At first, the speeches were free to play.

13 million+ subscribers across partner channels Claimed

If you already have an audience, a launch is just telling them where to go next.

4Slow, steady growth2021 to 2022

In March 2022 the app added Skills: you earn points and level up in eight life areas. By May 2022 it had passed 100,000 users and about 300,000 audio plays a month. That's roughly a year and a half after launch, even with a huge YouTube audience.

100,000 users Claimed

Even with a head start, expect years of building, not weeks.

5Alarms, streaks and subscriptions2023 to now

Today the app leads with an alarm clock that wakes you to a famous speech, plus streaks, widgets and leaderboards. It charges $4.99 a week, $9.99 a month or $59.99 a year. MRR means monthly recurring revenue: money that comes in every month from subscriptions. Mindset's is $24,251. A month's revenue can be higher, because it counts all money collected that month, including yearly plans paid up front. Revenue moves between about $16,000 and $35,000 a month.

$35,034 in Sep 2026, the best month shown Verified

Features that bring people back every day, like an alarm or a streak, are what keep a subscription alive.
