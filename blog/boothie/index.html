The story, phase by phase.

Each phase ends with one lesson you can use.

1A simpler app first?Apr 2025

In April 2025 a free iPhone app also called Boothie appeared: a plain vintage photobooth that takes three shots and makes a photo strip, with no AI. It came from a different developer account, and its code is public on GitHub. We couldn't confirm it is the same team, so treat this as a maybe.

0 US ratings Couldn't verify

A small free version is a cheap way to test a name and an idea before you add the expensive part.

2Launch into a trendJun 2026

The AI app launched on June 23, 2026. You pick a look, like a 2000s digicam flash, Kodak film or a vintage photobooth, take a selfie, and get it back in that style. The basic editor is free. The AI looks cost credits, through weekly subscriptions or credit packs. It earned $4,727 in its first week.

$4,727 in Jun 2026 Verified

When a look is already trending, a tool that makes that exact look in one tap is easy to explain.

3TikTok does the sellingJul to Sep 2026

The app is aimed at women 16 to 40 who post on TikTok and Instagram, and TikTok is its only listed marketing channel. A sale listing says growth came from TikTok photo carousels (posts you swipe through) posted by almost a dozen accounts in the US, Germany and Turkey. The website even tells visitors how to escape TikTok's built-in browser to reach the App Store. Revenue jumped to $27,283 in July and $43,716 in August.

$43,716 in Aug 2026 Verified

Show the before and after where your customer already scrolls. If your posts come from made-up characters, say so, or you risk losing trust and breaking the platform's rules.

4Android and the best monthSep to Oct 2026

An Android version came out on Google Play in September 2026, and a proper website went up by early October. October was the best month so far at $56,432.

$56,432 in Oct 2026 Verified

Once one platform works, the next one can add customers without new marketing.

5Selling the businessOct 2026 to now

On October 9, 2026, about 15 weeks after launch, Boothie was listed for sale on TrustMRR. The asking price there is $900,000, and it had received 32 offers. A different site lists it at $2,000,000. Trend apps can fade fast, so some founders sell while the numbers are high. In November the same developer account released a new app, a photo cleaner called Swipezy.

$900,000 asking price Claimed

An app is an asset: if you build something that earns, someone may pay you for it.
