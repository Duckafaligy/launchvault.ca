Rezi started as a resume template on Reddit and made $216K in October 2026

A free template, four slow years, then an early bet on AI writing. October 2026 brought in $216,471, read straight from Stripe. That is about 21% less than April 2026 ($275,551), and revenue has dropped every month since. Rezi's MRR (monthly recurring revenue) is $234,707: what all current subscriptions add up to per month, while $216,471 is the money actually collected in October.

AEO Engine turned an old Amazon ad agency into a $204K month by renaming itself for the AI search wave

It did not start from zero. An Amazon marketing agency from 2018 re-launched as an 'answer engine' agency in January 2026, just as brands started asking how to show up in ChatGPT. Its MRR (monthly recurring revenue: what clients pay every month) is $190,513, read straight from Stripe. Upscale B2B sold $3 email inboxes to cold-email agencies, then became one, and now makes about $99K a month

It started by selling the 'picks and shovels' of cold email (the tools everyone doing it needs): ready-to-send inboxes for a few dollars a month. Then it started running campaigns itself, with a free-to-try pilot. Its MRR (monthly recurring revenue: what customers pay every month) is $140,324, read straight from Stripe. That is higher than October's revenue, the money actually collected that month, so the two numbers don't match.

DM Champ sat near $10K a month for a year, then hit $28K after it changed who it sells to

It started by waking up one clinic's old leads on WhatsApp, found customers through cold email, AppSumo (a site that sells lifetime deals on software) and a webinar (a live online talk), and then turned itself into a tool agencies resell. Its MRR (monthly recurring revenue: what subscribers pay every month) is $135,927 today. That is far above any month's revenue so far, and we couldn't find out why.
