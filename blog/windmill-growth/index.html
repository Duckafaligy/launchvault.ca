The story, phase by phase

Each phase ends with one lesson you can use.

  1. 1

    A cheap first offer

    May to Aug 2024

    Windmill started by promoting founders' LinkedIn posts. The founder wrote the post, and Windmill made sure it got lots of likes and comments fast, so LinkedIn showed it to more people. That cost $500 a month. Writing the posts for you (ghostwriting) was the bigger plan at $1,800 a month. Don't copy the likes part: paying for likes and comments can break a platform's rules and makes people trust the post less. The useful idea is the cheap first offer.

    $500/mo and $1,800/moWaybackAug 14 2024

    LessonStart with a small, cheap offer that's easy to say yes to, then offer more to the people who stay.

  2. 2

    Bigger plans for bigger clients

    Late 2024 to early 2025

    By January 2025 the cheap plan was $650 a month and the top plans were $4,999 and $7,499, aimed at companies with $2M+ in yearly revenue. The homepage said 'Trusted by 100+ Founders and Executives' and '7 spots left for January'. A case studies page was archived in February 2025.

    $650 to $7,499 a monthWaybackJan 8 2025

    LessonOnce a cheap offer works, add a bigger version for customers who can pay more.

  3. 3

    A free tool as bait

    Mar to Aug 2025

    In April 2025 a podcast said Windmill had gone from zero to '$1M+ ARR in under a year', fully bootstrapped. ARR means yearly recurring revenue. A free 'AI Profile Optimizer' for LinkedIn went live around March 2025, and by August it was the banner at the top of the homepage.

    "$1M+ ARR in under a year"ClaimedApr 18 2025

    LessonA free tool that solves a tiny problem brings in people who might buy the big solution.

  4. 4

    Writing for Google too

    Feb to Apr 2026

    Windmill published dozens of guides like 'LinkedIn ghostwriter cost' and 'Best times to post on LinkedIn in 2026', the questions buyers type into Google. In April 2026 the homepage said it 'works with the top 0.1% of startup founders'. Monthly revenue stayed close to $100K.

    $101,149 in Apr 2026VerifiedNov 18 2026

    LessonWrite the answers to the exact questions your buyers search for.

  5. 5

    Steady growth, one main plan

    May to Nov 2026

    Revenue rose slowly from about $108K to $118K a month. The site now leads with 'Your LinkedIn brand. Without the AI accent.' and the most popular plan is $3,999 a month. It says it has worked with 185+ founders and keeps 30 on the books at a time.

    $118,047 in Oct 2026VerifiedNov 18 2026

    LessonWhen everyone uses AI to write, sounding like a real person becomes the thing you sell.
