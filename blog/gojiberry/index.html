The story, phase by phase.

Each phase ends with one lesson you can use.

0Done it beforeBefore 2025

The founders' earlier company, Coco AI, sold WhatsApp marketing to online shops. It reached about €500,000 a year in revenue through cold outreach and was sold to an American fund. So they already knew how to find customers by messaging them directly.

€500K ARR at the previous company Claimed

Skills carry over: what you learn selling one thing makes the next thing easier to sell.

1The waitlist that made $0Early 2025

Version one was an AI sales assistant that joined calls, took notes and filled in the CRM. The homepage asked people to join a waitlist, and it grew from 1,147 in April to over 2,000 in May. When it launched, none of them paid.

"Join 1147+ founders, sales professionals, and start ups already on the waitlist." Wayback

A signup is not a sale. Ask for money as early as you can.

2Rebuild in weeksJun to Jul 2025

The team threw out the note-taker and rebuilt the product from scratch around one job: find people on LinkedIn who are showing signs they want to buy, and hand over their contact details. By June the homepage said 'Trusted by 30+ B2B companies'. By July it said 100+.

100+ paying customers within 60 days of the rebuild Claimed

When nobody pays, change the product, not just the marketing.

3Reddit first2025

The co-founder posted stories on Reddit instead of ads: what $1,250 of LinkedIn influencer posts got him, and being turned down by Y Combinator after an interview. He warmed up each account for 1 to 2 weeks before posting and replied to every comment. He says Reddit brought 11 million views and 40,000+ visitors.

one post: about 2,000 visitors and 10 to 15 customers Claimed

Tell true stories with proof; a rejection can be your best post.

4Twelve channels at onceLate 2025

Once Reddit worked, the team added everything: 5,000 cold emails a day, about 800 LinkedIn messages a week from five accounts using its own tool, daily posts, five YouTube videos a week, SEO, newsletters, influencers and affiliates. Most customers were on a $99-a-month plan.

$30,017 revenue in Dec 2025 Verified

Use your own product to sell your product; it is the best demo you have.

5Y Combinator and fast growth2026

Gojiberry joined Y Combinator's Spring 2026 batch. Revenue roughly doubled every two to three months. The homepage count went from '500+ small sales teams' in March 2026 to '3,500+ growing B2B sales teams' in November.

$407,786 revenue in Jul 2026 Verified

Getting turned down once is not the end; apply again with better numbers.
