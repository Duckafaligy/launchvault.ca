Gojiberry's waitlist of 2,000 paid nothing. A rebuild took it to $407K in one month

The first product got a big waitlist and zero sales. The team rebuilt it in weeks, then pushed it through Reddit and a dozen other channels at once. Its MRR (monthly recurring revenue: what subscribers pay every month) was $424,368 at the last Stripe sync. A month's revenue is the money actually collected that month, so the two numbers don't match exactly.

The story, phase by phase

Each phase ends with one lesson you can use.

  1. 1

    Done it before

    Before 2025

    The founders' earlier company, Coco AI, sold WhatsApp marketing to online shops. It reached about €500,000 a year in revenue through cold outreach (messaging people who didn't know them yet) and was sold to an American fund. So they already knew how to find customers by messaging them directly.

    €500K ARR (yearly recurring revenue) at the previous companyClaimedDec 31 2025

    LessonSkills carry over: what you learn selling one thing makes the next thing easier to sell.

  2. 2

    The waitlist that made $0

    Early 2025

    Version one was an AI sales assistant that joined calls, took notes and filled in the CRM (the software sales teams use to track customers). The homepage asked people to join a waitlist, and it grew from 1,147 in April to over 2,000 in May. When it launched, none of them paid.

    "Join 1147+ founders, sales professionals, and start ups already on the waitlist."WaybackApr 1 2025

    LessonA signup is not a sale. Ask for money as early as you can.

  3. 3

    Rebuild in weeks

    Jun to Jul 2025

    The team threw out the note-taker and rebuilt the product from scratch around one job: find people on LinkedIn who are showing signs they want to buy, and hand over their contact details. By June the homepage said 'Trusted by 30+ B2B companies'. By July it said 100+.

    100+ paying customers within 60 days of the rebuildClaimedJul 24 2025

    LessonWhen nobody pays, change the product, not just the marketing.

  4. 4

    Reddit first

    2025

    The co-founder posted stories on Reddit instead of ads: what $1,250 of LinkedIn influencer posts got him, and being turned down by Y Combinator (a famous program that invests in startups) after an interview. He warmed up each account for 1 to 2 weeks before posting and replied to every comment. He says Reddit brought 11 million views and 40,000+ visitors.

    one post: about 2,000 visitors and 10 to 15 customersClaimedJan 2026

    LessonTell true stories with proof; a rejection can be your best post.

  5. 5

    Twelve channels at once

    Late 2025

    Once Reddit worked, the team added everything: 5,000 cold emails a day, about 800 LinkedIn messages a week from five accounts using its own tool, daily posts, five YouTube videos a week, SEO (getting found on Google), newsletters, influencers and affiliates (people paid a cut for each customer they bring). Most customers were on a $99-a-month plan.

    $30,017 revenue in Dec 2025VerifiedAug 14 2026

    LessonUse your own product to sell your product; it is the best demo you have.

  6. 6

    Y Combinator and fast growth

    2026

    Gojiberry joined Y Combinator's Spring 2026 batch. Revenue roughly doubled every two to three months. The homepage count went from '500+ small sales teams' in March 2026 to '3,500+ growing B2B sales teams' in November.

    $407,786 revenue in Jul 2026VerifiedAug 14 2026

    LessonGetting turned down once is not the end; apply again with better numbers.
