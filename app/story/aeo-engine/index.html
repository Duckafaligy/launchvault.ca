AEO Engine turned an old Amazon ad agency into a $204K month by renaming itself for the AI search wave

It did not start from zero. An Amazon marketing agency from 2018 re-launched as an 'answer engine' agency in January 2026, just as brands started asking how to show up in ChatGPT. Its MRR (monthly recurring revenue: what clients pay every month) is $190,513, read straight from Stripe.

Phase by phase

5 phases · each ends with one lesson
1

An Amazon agency

2018 to 2024

The business began as FosterFBA, which helped people sell on Amazon. In January 2019 its homepage offered product research, listing optimization and free tools. By 2023 it called itself a 'Data Driven Amazon Advertising Agency' and offered a free audit to get calls booked.

"Research, Optimize & Scale your Amazon FBA Business"WaybackWayback: fosterfba.com · Jan 21 2019

Lesson. A small service business can run for years and build skills you can carry into the next thing.

2

Spotting the new search

2025

People started asking ChatGPT and Google's AI answers instead of clicking search results. The company says it was already doing AI search work through 2025. By October 2025 aeoengine.ai was live, promising to get brands 'featured in ChatGPT, Google AI & Perplexity answers' and offering a free audit.

aeoengine.ai first archived Oct 30 2025WaybackWayback: aeoengine.ai · Oct 30 2025

Lesson. When the way people search changes, the people who already know the old way have a head start on the new one.

Lesson. When the way people search changes, the people who already know the old way have a head start on the new one.

3

A new name

Jan 2026

The company says it 'formalized' the AEO Engine brand around January 2026. AEO means answer engine optimization: making sure an AI recommends you when someone asks a question. That month a founder-story site listed it at $65,000 a month, which matches the Stripe numbers.

$65,126 in Jan 2026VerifiedTrustMRR: AEO Engine · Nov 18 2026

Lesson. A clear name for what you do can matter as much as the work itself.

4

Showing up everywhere

Mar to Apr 2026

In March 2026 it started a podcast about AI search, now billed as a daily show. In April the founder gave the AEO talk at a big Amazon seller conference in Nashville, where the agency's old clients gather. Revenue jumped to $98K in March, then settled around $75K to $77K a month.

$98,264 in Mar 2026VerifiedTrustMRR: AEO Engine · Nov 18 2026

Lesson. Go where your existing customers already are and teach them something new.

5

The jump

Sep to Oct 2026

Revenue went from about $80K in August to $164K in September and $204K in October. Plans are public: $1,597 or $2,997 a month, or custom from $10,000. In November it published an 'Answer Index' ranking how visible 100 law firms are in AI answers. We couldn't find a public reason for the September jump.

$204,043 in Oct 2026VerifiedTrustMRR: AEO Engine · Nov 18 2026

Lesson. Public prices and free reports let buyers say yes faster.
