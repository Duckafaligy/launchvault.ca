Stencil AI went from $16K to $63K a month in 2026 by fixing one boring job for tattoo artists

Two brothers, one of them a tattoo artist, built a tool that skips hours of hand-tracing. They charged from day one, sold on the web first, then put it on the App Store.

Phase by phase

5 phases · each ends with one lesson
1

A problem in the family

Before 2026

Before every tattoo, artists trace the design by hand into a clean line drawing called a stencil, then transfer it onto skin. The team behind Stencil AI is two brothers. One is the programmer, the other is a tattoo artist, so the problem was sitting right next to the person who could code a fix.

"Two passionate brothers"WaybackWayback Machine: stencilai.app, Feb 1 2026 · Feb 1 2026

Lesson. The best problems to solve are ones a friend or family member deals with every week.

2

Web first, paid from the start

Sep 2025 to Feb 2026

The website was already online by September 2025. In February 2026 the homepage promised "a perfect stencil in 30 seconds" and there was no free plan: $14, $29 or $69 a month. The phone app was still marked "Coming soon". In January 2026 it already brought in about $16,000.

$16,032 in Jan 2026VerifiedTrustMRR: Stencil AI · Nov 18 2026

Lesson. You don't need an app store listing to start charging. A simple website with a price tests whether people will pay.

2

Web first, paid from the start

Sep 2025 to Feb 2026

The website was already online by September 2025. In February 2026 the homepage promised "a perfect stencil in 30 seconds" and there was no free plan: $14, $29 or $69 a month. The phone app was still marked "Coming soon". In January 2026 it already brought in about $16,000.

$16,032 in Jan 2026VerifiedTrustMRR: Stencil AI · Nov 18 2026

Lesson. You don't need an app store listing to start charging. A simple website with a price tests whether people will pay.

3

Selling to professionals

2026

Stencil AI is sold to people who earn money with it. A Studio plan charges per artist, so a whole tattoo shop can pay for it. The pitch is time: the October 2026 homepage says it "replaces hours of tracing in Procreate".

$29/month Pro, $69/month StudioWaybackWayback Machine: stencilai.app, Oct 11 2026 · Oct 11 2026

Lesson. People pay more easily for a tool that saves them time at work than for one that is just fun.

4

The App Store launch

Mar to Jul 2026

The iPhone app went live on March 12, 2026, with an Android version too. Monthly revenue grew every month from January to July, from about $16,000 to about $62,000.

$62,455 in Jul 2026VerifiedTrustMRR: Stencil AI · Nov 18 2026

Lesson. Once the website proves people pay, an app puts the tool where the customer works: on a phone or tablet at the chair.

5

Holding steady and getting found

Aug 2026 to now

Since July, revenue has stayed between about $60,000 and $64,000 a month. The site has a blog of how-to guides, like how to print a stencil or make one in Procreate, so artists searching Google land on it. The TrustMRR listing names app store search, SEO, a blog, TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest and YouTube as its channels.

$62,976 in Oct 2026VerifiedTrustMRR: Stencil AI · Nov 18 2026

Lesson. Write the guide your customer is already searching for, and put your tool inside it.
