Upscale B2B sold $3 email inboxes to cold-email agencies, then became one, and now makes about $99K a month

It started by selling the 'picks and shovels' of cold email: ready-to-send inboxes for a few dollars a month. Then it started running campaigns itself, with a free-to-try pilot. Its MRR (monthly recurring revenue: what customers pay every month) is $140,324, read straight from Stripe.

Phase by phase

4 phases · each ends with one lesson
1

Fixing their own problem

2023 to 2024

Cold email means emailing businesses that don't know you yet. To send a lot without landing in spam, agencies need many 'warmed up' inboxes. The company's site says it had been burned by bad inbox providers, 'That's why we started Upscale B2B'. TrustMRR lists March 2023 as the founding date.

Founded Mar 31 2023ClaimedTrustMRR: Upscale System LLC (markdown profile) · Nov 19 2026

Lesson. A problem that annoys you at work is often a problem other people will pay to make go away.

2

Selling inboxes to agencies

Dec 2024 to 2025

The site sold Google Workspace and Microsoft inboxes, set up and warmed, for $2 to $7 a month each, with a free domain. Bigger buyers got lower prices: $2.50 each at 100 inboxes, $2 at 1,000. A sales calculator showed how many deals more inboxes could bring.

$2 to $7 per inbox per monthWaybackWayback: upscaleb2b.com · May 19 2025

Lesson. Selling to businesses that already spend money on a problem is easier than convincing someone they have one.

Selling inboxes to agencies

Lesson. Selling to businesses that already spend money on a problem is easier than convincing someone they have one.

3

A separate brand for inboxes

Apr to Nov 2025

The inbox business got its own site, warminboxes.com, first archived in April 2025 with 'Get FREE Warmed-Up Domains'. By November 2025 its headline promised '90%+ Deliverability'. The founder's TrustMRR account is named after it.

"Get FREE Warmed-Up Domains"WaybackWayback: warminboxes.com · Apr 18 2025

Lesson. If one product sells itself, give it its own name and page so people can find it.

4

Running campaigns, try first

Jan 2026 to now

By January 2026 upscaleb2b.com led with done-for-you lead generation and a trial: 'the only provider offering a trial for fully managed lead generation'. It runs a small pilot campaign first, then grows it if it works. Revenue went from about $34K in December 2025 to $99K in October 2026.

$99,409 in Oct 2026VerifiedTrustMRR: Upscale System LLC · Nov 18 2026

Lesson. Letting customers try before they commit can win clients who would never sign a big contract cold.
