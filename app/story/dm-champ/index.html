DM Champ sat near $10K a month for a year, then hit $28K after it changed who it sells to

It started by waking up one clinic's old leads on WhatsApp, found customers through cold email, AppSumo and a webinar, and then turned itself into a tool agencies resell. Its MRR (monthly recurring revenue: what subscribers pay every month) is $135,927 today.

Phase by phase

6 phases · each ends with one lesson
1

The problem at work

Before 2023

The founder ran a marketing agency and did the marketing for a hair transplant clinic. He saw clinics losing leads because nobody answered WhatsApp, Instagram and website chats fast enough. That became the idea.

Company founded Nov 7 2022 (AppSumo) or Jan 2023 (Starter Story)Sources differStarter Story interview · Mar 20 2026

Lesson. The best ideas often come from a problem you watch people have every day.

2

Wake up old leads

2023 to 2024

The founder cold-emailed local clinics using a tool called Instantly. Three were interested and he followed up with one. That clinic had fewer than 200 old leads; the AI messaged them on WhatsApp and the clinic started paying every month. The product was first called Whatz.ai, and in 2024 the homepage still sold exactly this.

€2,000 a month from the first clinicClaimedStarter Story interview · Mar 20 2026

Lesson. Start with a result a business can see in money, like customers who come back.

2

Wake up old leads

2023 to 2024

The founder cold-emailed local clinics using a tool called Instantly. Three were interested and he followed up with one. That clinic had fewer than 200 old leads; the AI messaged them on WhatsApp and the clinic started paying every month. The product was first called Whatz.ai, and in 2024 the homepage still sold exactly this.

€2,000 a month from the first clinicClaimedStarter Story interview · Mar 20 2026

Lesson. Start with a result a business can see in money, like customers who come back.

3

A cheap launch with lots of refunds

2024

DM Champ went on AppSumo, a site that sells lifetime deals on software. It made about €20,000 from 300 customers, but 40% asked for refunds. The founder blames the cheapest tier, which 'attracted tire-kickers'.

€20K, 300 customers, 40% refundsClaimedStarter Story interview · Mar 20 2026

Lesson. Price too low and you attract people who were never going to use it.

4

One webinar, and ads that backfired

Not public

A business coach the team knew ran a 60-minute webinar for 300 people. The founder says 'we had $20K in Stripe' two minutes after the offer went live, from 80 customers. Meta ads closed 15% of leads, but those customers cancelled after three months.

$20K from one webinarClaimedStarter Story interview · Mar 20 2026

Lesson. Someone else's audience that already trusts them can sell more in an hour than months of ads.

5

Stuck around $10K

2025 to Sep 2026

In mid-2025 the homepage dared visitors: 'Find Us an AI Sales Agent More Human than Ours and We'll Send You $100'. In March 2026 the founder told Starter Story the business made about $10,000 a month. Stripe agrees: monthly revenue stayed between $6,241 and $12,263 from December 2025 to September 2026.

$10,310 in Mar 2026VerifiedTrustMRR: DM Champ · Nov 18 2026

Lesson. A clever headline gets attention, but it doesn't change who your customer is.

6

Sell to agencies instead

2026

By July 2026 the homepage spoke to agencies: put your own logo on the AI and resell it to your clients. The founder calls the 2026 AppSumo launch its third, and says 'What we used to make in a year, we now make in a month.' Stripe revenue nearly tripled in October 2026.

$28,426 revenue in Oct 2026VerifiedTrustMRR: DM Champ · Nov 18 2026

Lesson. Sometimes the fix is a new buyer, not a new feature: sell to the people who sell to everyone else.
