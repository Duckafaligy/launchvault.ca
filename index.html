      Questions

      The things students and parents ask before signing up.

      Where do the numbers come from?

      Revenue comes from TrustMRR, which reads it straight from Stripe or RevenueCat. We add GitHub, the Wayback Machine, interviews and the founder's own posts, and link every source with a date.

      What does Verified mean?

      Read from a live source, like Stripe through TrustMRR, on the date shown. Claimed means the founder or an interview said it. Sources differ means two sources disagree, and we show both.

      Why do some numbers have old dates?

      We show the date a number was read, not today's date. Gojiberry's revenue was last updated on TrustMRR on Aug 14 2026, so that's the date you see.

      Do I need money or an audience to use this?

      No. The stories are picked so you can copy a move with little money and no followers. Each breakdown ends with a checklist that fits around school.

      I'm under 18. Can I use it?

      Yes, it's made for 15 to 20. Free needs only an email. If you're under 18, a parent pays for Full.

      What does Full include?

      Every breakdown, old and new, a new breakdown every week, your personal path from the quiz, saved checklists and breakdown requests. It's $5 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly.

      How is it different from YouTube?

      Videos are great for stories, but the numbers are rarely checked. Each breakdown labels every number, links the source with a date, and takes about 10 minutes to read.

      Is this financial advice?

      No. LaunchVault explains what real founders did, using public sources. Nothing here is financial or investment advice.
