      Questions, answered.

      What students and parents ask us before signing up.

      Where do your numbers actually come from?

      Revenue comes from TrustMRR, which reads it straight from Stripe or RevenueCat. We add GitHub, the Wayback Machine, interviews and the founder's own posts, and link every source with the date we read it.

      What's the difference between Verified and Claimed?

      Verified means we read it from a live source, like Stripe through TrustMRR, on the date shown. Claimed means the founder or an interview said it, and we link where. Sources differ means two sources disagree, so we show both.

      Why do some numbers have old dates?

      We show the date a number was read, not today's date. Gojiberry's revenue was last updated on TrustMRR on Aug 14 2026, so that's the date you see.

      Do I need money or followers to get something out of this?

      No. The stories are picked so you can copy a move with little money and no audience. Each breakdown ends with a checklist that fits around school.

      I'm under 18. Can I still use it?

      Yes, it's made for 15 to 20 year olds. Free needs only an email. If you're under 18, a parent pays for Pro or Builder.

      What do I get with Pro and Builder?

      Pro gives you every breakdown, old and new, the checklist for every story saved to your account, and your quiz path. It's $5 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly. Builder adds templates and worksheets for each story, breakdown requests and early access, all coming soon, for $12 a month or $10 billed yearly. Prices are placeholders until launch.

      How is this different from watching YouTube?

      Videos are great for stories, but the numbers are rarely checked. Each breakdown labels every number, links the source with a date, and takes about 10 minutes to read.

      Is any of this financial advice?

      No. LaunchVault explains what real founders did, using public sources. Nothing here is financial or investment advice.
