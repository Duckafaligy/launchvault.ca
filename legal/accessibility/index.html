01Our goal

We want everyone to be able to read LaunchVault, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, zoom or other assistive tools. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, which is also the standard Ontario's accessibility law (AODA) points to.

02What we do

  • Every page works with a keyboard, and there's a "Skip to content" link at the top.
  • Focus is easy to see when you move through the page with Tab.
  • Every number has a text label (Verified, Claimed and so on), not just a colour.
  • Every chart has a "Show as table" button or a data table that screen readers can read. Small trend lines read out their numbers.
  • On revenue charts, you can reach each founder move with the keyboard from the moves list, and screen readers announce the readout as it changes.
  • Text colours passed an automated contrast check against WCAG AA on 12 pages.
  • Phone menus and the search panel keep keyboard focus inside while they're open. Esc closes them and puts focus back where you were.
  • Animations calm down or stop when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Pages are built and checked for phone, tablet and desktop screens.
  • The cookie card can be used with a keyboard and closes with Esc.

03Known gaps

  • We haven't tested by hand with a screen reader like NVDA or VoiceOver yet.
  • We haven't had an outside accessibility audit yet. [Planned date.]
  • The contrast check was automated and covered 12 pages, not every page or every state.
  • Company logos come from their owners and may not have strong contrast.

04Tell us

If something is hard to use, or you need a page in another format, email [contact email] with the page link and what went wrong. We'll reply within [5] business days and tell you what we'll fix and when.
