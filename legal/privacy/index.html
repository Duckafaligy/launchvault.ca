Privacy policy

Draft · November 17, 2026

Draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer. This is a plain-English first draft of how LaunchVault plans to handle your information. It will be reviewed before launch, and it may change.

ContentsWho we areWhat we collectPaymentsWhat we don't doYour rightsChangesContact
The short version
  • We collect your email, and an account only if you choose to make one.
  • Payments go through Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • We don't sell your data, and you can ask us to delete it.

Who we are

LaunchVault publishes breakdowns of real company success stories. In this policy, "we" means LaunchVault.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you sign up for the weekly email or an account.
  • A password, if you create an account. Passwords are stored hashed, never as plain text.
  • Your quiz answers stay on your device. If you make an account, we save them so your path follows you.
  • Basic usage data, like which pages are opened, to see what's useful.

This preview doesn't collect anything yet: the sign-up forms don't send data anywhere.

Payments

If you or a parent pay for Full, the payment is handled by Stripe. We receive whether the payment worked and the plan, not your full card number. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian pays.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't show ads based on your personal information.
  • We don't share your quiz answers with anyone.

Your rights

Under Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA) you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it. We'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell account holders by email.
