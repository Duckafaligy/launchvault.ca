Privacy policy

Draft · Last updated November 19, 2026

Draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer. This is a plain-English template of how LaunchVault plans to handle your information. It will be reviewed before launch, and it may change. Text in [square brackets] is still to be filled in.

The short version
  • Right now nothing you do on LaunchVault is sent to us. Your ticks, progress and quiz answers stay on your device.
  • When accounts open, we keep your email, age range, progress and quiz answers, in Canada.
  • If you're under 13, the app stays closed. We keep nothing about you, only a marker on your device that keeps it closed.
  • No ads, no trackers, no selling your data. You can download or delete your data at any time.

Who we are

LaunchVault publishes breakdowns of real company success stories for people aged about 15 to 20. In this policy, "LaunchVault", "we" and "us" mean the operator of launchvault-site.vercel.app and the LaunchVault app. [Legal name of the business, if different.]

We are based in Ontario, Canada. Canada's federal privacy law, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), applies to how we handle personal information. Ontario has no separate private-sector privacy law that replaces it, so PIPEDA is the main law for us. Our privacy contact is listed under Contact.

Right now (preview)

LaunchVault is a preview. Accounts are optional and there are no payments. That means:

  • Without an account, your progress, checklist ticks, age range and quiz answers are saved only in your browser's storage on this device.
  • If you make an account, what's listed below is saved with it so it follows you to other devices.
  • If you send a "Report a mistake" note or answer the questions at the end of a story, that text is sent to us.
  • We run no analytics and no ad trackers.

What we collect

On your device (now): what you do in the app, like checklist ticks, how far you've read each story, your age range, your quiz answers and path, your email-preference switches, and the date you shared the parent link. The full list of storage keys is in the cookie policy.

When you make an account:

  • Your email address. Sign-in uses Clerk. You can continue with Google, GitHub, Apple or Discord, or get a one-time code by email. We keep your email address and the first and last name you give at sign-up. We never get your photo or contacts.
  • Your display name, if you use the community: by default your first name and last initial (for example "Maya K"). Other members can see it, including members under 18. You can change it in Settings.
  • Your age range (13 to 15, 16 to 17, 18 to 20, or 21 or older). The app asks your birth year once to work this out. The year itself is never saved, and we never ask for your birthday.
  • Your progress: which checklist items you ticked and how far you read each story. Progress you save to an account is stored with Supabase.
  • Your quiz answers and the path they built.
  • Your email preferences, like the "Weekly breakdown" email. Every email is off until you switch it on.

The sign-in provider you pick may show Clerk your profile picture while you sign in. We don't receive or keep it. We never see your Google, GitHub, Apple or Discord password.

From anyone who visits: our host, Vercel, receives the basic details every website gets, like your IP address, browser type and the page asked for, to deliver the page and keep the site secure. Your browser also asks Google Fonts for our fonts, which shares your IP address with Google.

From under 13s: nothing. See Under 18 and parents.

Why we use it

  • To run your account: to sign you in and keep your progress the same on every device.
  • To show you the right path: your quiz answers pick which stories and checklists come first.
  • To apply the age rules: your age range decides whether a parent needs to pay for a plan.
  • To email you only what you switched on. Every email will have an unsubscribe link, as Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) requires.
  • To keep the site safe and working: basic server logs help us stop abuse and fix errors.

We only use your information for these reasons. If we ever want to use it for something new, we'll ask first.

Where it's stored

  • Supabase holds our database (email, age range, progress, quiz answers, email preferences), in its Canada region (ca-central-1, Montreal).
  • Clerk runs sign-in. It is based in the United States and stores sign-in details there.
  • Vercel hosts the website. It serves pages from data centres around the world, so request logs may be handled outside Canada.
  • Google Fonts serves our fonts.
  • Stripe will handle payments when they open. We will never see or store a full card number.

When information is handled outside Canada, the laws of that country apply to it, and their authorities may be able to access it. We pick providers that protect data with contracts and security standards. [List each provider's data processing agreement once signed.]

How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you delete it. Your cookie choice is kept for 12 months, then we ask again.
  • Your account data: while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete it within 30 days, and from backups within [backup period, for example 30] more days.
  • If an account is unused for [2] years, we'll email you, then delete it if you don't reply.
  • Server logs: a short time, set by Vercel, for security and debugging.
  • Payment records (later): as long as tax law requires, which in Canada is usually 6 years.

Under 18 and parents

  • Under 13: you can read the public site, but we don't store anything about you. The app asks your birth year the first time you open it. If the year means you could be under 13, the app stays closed, clears anything it saved on your device and never sends anything to our database. It keeps one marker on your device (lv-age-block, for 12 months) so the app stays closed; the marker doesn't hold your birth year. If we learn we have information from someone under 13, we delete it.
  • 13 to 17: you can use LaunchVault and make an account (when accounts open). A paid plan needs a parent or guardian to pay.
  • Parent pays: in the app you can copy a link to our plans, or open your own email app to send it to a parent. We don't send that email and we don't store your parent's email address. We only keep the date you shared the link, on your device. When payments open, your parent pays through Stripe, and we'll keep the parent's email only to send receipts.
  • Parents can ask to see or delete their child's data at any time. Write to us at the address under Contact.

Community chat (coming soon)

When the community opens, this is what it means for your information. The rules for what you can post are in the Community Guidelines.

  • What others see. Your display name (first name and last initial by default, with a 4-digit tag), what you post, and your vote in a poll. Members under 18 can see this too, and their names are visible to you.
  • Moderation. Every message is checked automatically for things like harassment, sexual content and personal details, and may be checked by a person. An AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) helps sort messages; people make the decisions on bans. Reports and moderation actions are logged.
  • Links. Links you post are checked against a safety service after you post them. Opening a link takes you to another site with its own privacy rules; we show a warning first.
  • Images (not switched on yet). Images are re-saved on our side to remove hidden details like location, and scanned before anyone else can see them, including a check against lists of known child sexual abuse material. Suspected abuse material is kept only as the law requires for reporting to the authorities (Cybertip.ca in Canada).
  • How long. Removed messages and images are kept for up to 90 days for safety reviews, then deleted. "Delete your data" in Account removes your posts' text, votes and images, except material we must keep by law.

Cookies and storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We use your browser's local storage for things the site needs, like your ticks and progress, and for preferences you can turn off. Sign-in will add a few cookies from Clerk when it's turned on. The cookie policy lists every one and what it does. You can change your choice any time with Cookie settings.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information, or share it for advertising.
  • We don't show ads based on your personal information.
  • We don't share your quiz answers with anyone.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • Download your data. Right now: open Account in the app and choose "Download your data". It saves everything on this device as a file. With an account, ask us and we'll send what we hold.
  • Delete your data. Right now: "Delete your data" on the same Account page clears everything LaunchVault saved on this device. With an account, delete it from Account or ask us.
  • Correct your data, for example your email or age range.
  • Withdraw consent, for example turn off emails or Preferences storage.

We'll reply within 30 days, and we may need to check it's really you first. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

EU, UK and California

EU and UK (GDPR and UK GDPR). We use your information because it's needed to provide the service you asked for (your account and progress), because of our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure, and with your consent for emails and Preferences storage. You also have the right to restrict or object to how we use your data and to take it to another service. You can complain to your local data protection authority, or in the UK to the Information Commissioner's Office. [Name an EU or UK representative here if one is legally required.]

California (CCPA, as amended by the CPRA). You have the right to know what we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We don't sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. If we ever add analytics, we'll treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a "no".

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is important, we'll tell account holders by email before it applies.
