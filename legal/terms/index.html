The short version

  • Breakdowns are for learning. They are not financial advice.
  • You need to be 13 or older to make an account. Under 18? A parent pays for a paid plan.
  • Every number has a label and a date. If one is wrong, tell us and we'll fix it in the open.
  • Logos belong to their owners. Showing one doesn't mean a partnership.

01Using LaunchVault

These terms are an agreement between you and LaunchVault ("we", "us"). By using the website or the app, you agree to them. If you're under 18, please read them with a parent or guardian.

LaunchVault is a preview right now. Accounts are optional, there are no payments, and features may change or stop without notice while we build.

02Age rules

  • Under 13: you can read the public site and the blog, but you can't use the app or make an account. The app keeps only a marker on your device so it stays closed.
  • 13 to 17: you can make a free account (when accounts open). A parent or guardian must agree to these terms for you and pay for any paid plan.
  • 18 and over: you can make an account and pay for a plan yourself.

Please tell us your real birthday. If we learn an account breaks these rules, we may close it and delete its data.

03Not financial advice

Breakdowns explain what happened at real companies, from public sources. They are for learning only. They are not financial, legal, tax or business advice, and they are not a promise that you'll get the same results. Before you spend money, invest or start a business, talk to someone you trust, and a qualified professional where it matters.

05Accuracy and corrections

Every number on LaunchVault carries a label and the date we read it:

  • Verified: read from payments (Stripe or RevenueCat) through TrustMRR.
  • Live: counted by the platform itself, like GitHub stars.
  • Wayback: a dated snapshot saved on the Wayback Machine.
  • Claimed: what a founder said, linked and dated.
  • Sources differ: two sources disagree, so we show both.
  • Couldn't verify: no source we could check, or it stopped updating.

Numbers change after we read them, and sources can be wrong. A label tells you how sure we are, not that a number is still true today. See how we check numbers.

How to report a mistake: email [contact email] with the page link, the number you think is wrong and, if you have one, a source. We'll reply within [7] days. If we got it wrong, we fix the page and add a dated note saying what changed and why. We don't quietly edit numbers.

06Acceptable use

Please don't:

  • copy, scrape or download breakdowns in bulk, or republish or resell them;
  • share your account, or get around a paid plan;
  • try to break, overload or get into parts of the site you shouldn't, or test its security without our written OK;
  • use LaunchVault to harass anyone, pretend to be someone else, or break the law;
  • make accounts with bots or scripts.

If you do, we may suspend or close your account. Found a security problem? Tell us at [contact email] and we'll thank you.

07Community rules (coming soon)

Community chat is also covered by our Community Guidelines, which explain these rules with examples, how moderation works, and how to report or appeal.

The community is for people aged 13 and up. By posting you agree to:

  • Be kind. Argue about ideas, never about the person.
  • Keep it here: no contact details, no "DM me", and no links to chat apps or social profiles. There are no private messages.
  • Links are allowed, but you're responsible for what you share. No scams, no adult or gambling sites, no shortened links. We warn people before they open a link, and we haven't checked what's on the other side.
  • Images (when switched on): screenshots and photos only. Nothing sexual, violent or hateful, no one else's face or personal details, and no passwords or keys. Images are scanned before anyone sees them.
  • No selling, spam or impersonation, including posing as LaunchVault or a moderator.
  • Report, don't argue. We can remove posts, pause or ban accounts, and report illegal content to the authorities.

08Our content and yours

Breakdowns, charts, checklists, text and design are ours, or used with permission. You can share links and short quotes with credit to LaunchVault. Please don't copy whole breakdowns.

What you put in, like your ticks, stays yours. You let us store and use it only to run LaunchVault for you, as the privacy policy explains.

Quotes from founders, interviews and other sources belong to their authors. We quote short parts to report on and comment on them, and we link to the original.

09Logos and names

Company names, logos and trademarks belong to their owners. We show them to say which company a story is about and where a number was read. It doesn't mean they made, checked or endorse LaunchVault, or that we have a partnership. If you own a logo and want it changed or removed, email [contact email].

We work hard to get things right, but LaunchVault is provided "as is". As far as the law allows, we aren't responsible for losses that come from relying on a breakdown, from the site being unavailable, or from websites we link to. If we are responsible for something, our total liability is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before. Nothing here removes rights you have under consumer protection law that can't be removed.
