The short version

  • Right now LaunchVault sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage, and everything stays on this device.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Preferences storage is optional. Necessary storage keeps your ticks and progress working.

01The basics

A cookie is a small file a website saves in your browser. Local storage and session storage do the same job, but they are never sent to a server: only the page itself can read them. LaunchVault uses local storage for nearly everything, so your progress stays on your device unless you make an account.

02Your choice

The first time you visit, a small card asks what you allow. You can pick "Accept all", "Only necessary", or "Choose" to set each kind yourself. We remember your choice for 12 months, then ask again.

05Analytics (off)

We don't use any analytics right now, so there is nothing to list. The switch stays off. If we ever add analytics, we'll update this page and ask you again before anything runs.

06When sign-in is turned on

Clerk runs sign-in and sets these necessary cookies when you use it. Exact names can carry a short suffix; we'll confirm the final list when it goes live.

NameSet byWhat it doesHow long
__sessionClerkA short-lived token that proves you're signed in.About a minute, renewed while you use the site

Supabase sets no cookies or storage of its own. When you're signed in, the app passes Clerk's token to Supabase to save your progress; Supabase doesn't keep a separate login in your browser.

07Other services

Your browser loads our fonts from Google Fonts, which sets no cookies for this but does see your IP address. When sign-in is on, the sign-in code is loaded from the jsDelivr CDN. If you choose Google, GitHub, Apple or Discord to sign in, that company's own cookies apply on its sign-in page.

08Clearing it

  • In the app, open Account and choose "Delete your data" to clear everything LaunchVault saved on this device.
  • You can also clear site data in your browser's settings. Your ticks and progress will be gone unless you have an account.
